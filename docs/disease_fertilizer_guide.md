# Disease-to-Fertilizer & Nutrient Management Guide

> **Authoritative agronomic reference linking plant disease status to fertilizer selection, nutrient adjustments, and organic soil amendments for Indian agriculture.**
> *Sourced from ICAR, TNAU Agritech Portal, PAU Package of Practices, and CPRI Shimla.*

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

In Indian farming practice, applying generic chemical fertilizers without knowing the crop's disease status often proves disastrous:
* **The "Urea Trap" in Blights**: When farmers observe yellowing or blighting leaves (e.g. Potato/Tomato Late Blight), the common mistake is broadcasting extra Urea (Nitrogen). This spikes free amino acids in the leaf apoplast, softening epidermal cell walls and allowing *Phytophthora* and *Alternaria* fungal hyphae to consume the crop within 48 hours.
* **The Protective Shield of Potash and Calcium**: Adequate Potassium ($K_2O$) and Calcium ($Ca^{2+}$) promote the synthesis of lignin, suberin, and calcium pectate in the plant cell wall middle lamella, physically impeding fungal appressoria and bacterial invasion.
* **Micronutrient Resilience in Viral Infections**: For viral diseases (e.g., Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus), chemical fertilizers cannot kill the virus, but foliar applications of Zinc ($Zn$), Iron ($Fe$), Boron ($B$), and Potassium restore chlorophyll synthesis, reduce vein stunting, and salvage economic fruit yield.

## 2. Key Fertilizers Used in Indian Agriculture & Their Agronomic Roles

| Fertilizer Name | Grade / Composition | Primary Agronomic Role & Disease Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neem-Coated Urea (NCU)** | 46% Nitrogen ($N$) | Primary vegetative growth. **Must be reduced/halted during fungal & bacterial outbreaks.** |
| **Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP)** | 18% $N$, 46% $P_2O_5$ | Basal root establishment. Phosphorus enhances systemic acquired resistance (SAR). |
| **Muriate of Potash (MOP)** | 60% $K_2O$ ($KCl$) | Enhances cell wall rigidity, stomatal regulation, and disease resistance. |
| **Potassium Sulphate (SOP)** | 50% $K_2O$ + 17.5% $S$ | Chloride-free potash. **Superior to MOP during blight outbreaks and for potato tuber quality.** |
| **Single Super Phosphate (SSP)** | 16% $P_2O_5$ + 11% $S$ + 19% $Ca$ | Provides calcium and sulphur alongside phosphorus. Excellent basal fertilizer for disease resilience. |
| **NPK 10:26:26 / 12:32:16** | Complex balanced | High potash and phosphorus formulations; ideal basal dose for disease-prone soils. |
| **Water Soluble MKP (0:52:34)** | 52% $P_2O_5$ + 34% $K_2O$ | Foliar spray. **Actively suppresses powdery mildews, rusts, and downy mildews.** |
| **Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45 / Multi-K)** | 13% $N$ + 45% $K_2O$ | Foliar spray. Induces rapid drought and pathogen resistance during reproductive stages. |
| **Calcium Nitrate** | 15.5% $N$ + 18.8% $Ca$ | Foliar or fertigation. Pectin cross-linker in fruit skin; prevents blossom end rot and bacterial spots. |
| **Zinc Sulphate ($ZnSO_4$)** | 21% or 33% $Zn$ | Essential enzyme activator. Alleviates viral stunting and maize leaf spot susceptibility. |
| **Neem Cake** | Organic amendment | Triterpenoids inhibit nitrification and suppress soil nematodes and fungal inocula. |
| **Trichoderma viride / harzianum** | Bio-fungicide | Colonizes rhizosphere; produces antibiotics (gliotoxin/viridin) that lyse pathogen hyphae. |

## 4. How the ML and Knowledge Base Layers Collaborate

In `src/recommend.py`:
1. **Machine Learning Model (Random Forest / Logistic Regression / SVM)**:
   Takes environmental and soil inputs (`N`, `P`, `K`, `Temperature`, `Humidity`, `Soil Moisture`, `Soil Type`, `Crop Type`) and predicts the standard agronomic baseline fertilizer (e.g. Urea, DAP, 10-26-26).
2. **Disease-Aware Rule Engine**:
   Checks the predicted leaf disease from the vision pipeline. If a disease is detected, it intercepts the baseline recommendation:
   * Displays an immediate agronomic warning (e.g. *"ALERT: Phytophthora infestans detected — immediately suspend Urea application"*).
   * Adjusts the dosage and suggests therapeutic foliar supplements (e.g. 0:52:34 MKP, Calcium Nitrate, SOP).
   * Provides certified bio-control measures (e.g. *Trichoderma viride*, Neem cake).
   * Quotes the ICAR / TNAU research reference.
